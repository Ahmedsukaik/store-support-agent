# Talking to our team

## Support hours
Our support team works Monday to Friday, 8 am to 6 pm Pacific time.
Messages sent outside those hours are answered the next business day.

## What the chat assistant can do
The chat assistant answers questions about orders, products and our policies.
It can start returns, exchanges, cancellations and address changes, and a person on our team reviews each one.
You can ask for a person at any time.
