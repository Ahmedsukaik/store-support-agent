# Damaged, defective or wrong items

## Damaged in transit
If an item arrives damaged, tell us within 7 days of delivery and send a photo of the item and the box.
Our team arranges a free replacement or a full refund. You do not need to send the damaged item back unless we ask.

## Defective items
If an item is defective within 30 days of delivery, you can return it for free for a refund or a replacement.
After 30 days, defects are covered by our 2-year warranty.

## Wrong item
If we sent the wrong item, the return is free and we send you the item you ordered.
