# Changing or cancelling an order

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order until it ships. Once an order has shipped, it cannot be cancelled, but you can return it after it arrives.

## Changing the shipping address
You can change the shipping address until the order ships. The new address must be in the United States or Canada.

## Changing the items
We cannot add, remove or swap items in an order that has been placed. If the order has not shipped, you can cancel it and place a new one.

## Who confirms changes
A person on our team confirms every cancellation and address change, usually within 2 business hours.
