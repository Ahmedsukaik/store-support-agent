# Payments and prices

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Copperleaf gift cards.

## Price adjustments
If an item you bought drops in price within 14 days of your order, we refund the difference once per item.
Our team handles price adjustment requests.

## Discount codes
You can use one discount code per order. A code cannot be added after the order is placed.
