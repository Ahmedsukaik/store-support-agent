# Returns and exchanges

## Return window
You can return items within 30 days of delivery.
Items must be unused, with the original tags attached.

## Final sale items
Socks and fuel canisters are final sale and cannot be returned.
Socks are final sale for hygiene reasons. Fuel canisters cannot be shipped back.

## Return shipping fee
For returns because you changed your mind or ordered the wrong size, a return shipping fee of $6.95 is deducted from your refund.
Returns are free when an item is defective, was damaged in transit, or is not the item you ordered.
Exchanges are always free.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after your return arrives at our warehouse.
The original shipping cost is refunded only if the item was defective or we sent the wrong item.

## Exchanges
You can exchange an item for a different size of the same product, if that size is in stock.
If the size you want is out of stock, we can refund the item instead.

## How returns are approved
A person on our team reviews every return request, usually within one business day.
Once your return is approved, we email you a prepaid return label.
