# Shipping and delivery

## Where we ship
We ship to addresses in the United States and Canada. We do not ship to other countries yet.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on orders of $75 or more. Below $75, standard shipping costs $6.95.
Express shipping costs $14.95, and it is free on orders of $250 or more.

## Delivery times
Orders placed before 2 pm Pacific time on a business day leave our warehouse the same day.
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days.
You get a tracking number by email as soon as your order ships.

## Late orders
If your order is past its estimated delivery date, it usually arrives within 2 business days.
If it is more than 3 business days late, our team asks the carrier to trace it.

## Lost parcels
If the tracking has not updated for 7 days, we treat the parcel as lost.
Our team opens a trace with the carrier and sends you a free replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.
You do not need to wait for the trace to finish.
