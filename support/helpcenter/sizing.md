# Sizing and fit

## Jackets
The Northline Rain Jacket fits true to size, with room for a fleece underneath.
If you are between two sizes and plan to wear layers, choose the larger size.

## Backpacks
The Trailhead 40L comes in two torso sizes. S/M fits torso lengths from 40 to 48 cm. M/L fits torso lengths from 46 to 54 cm.

## Socks
Sock size S fits US shoe sizes 5 to 7.5, size M fits 8 to 10.5, and size L fits 11 to 13.
