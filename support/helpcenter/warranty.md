# Warranty

## What is covered
All Copperleaf gear has a 2-year warranty against defects in materials and workmanship, counted from the delivery date.

## What is not covered
The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, accidents, misuse, or items that were altered.

## How to make a claim
A person on our team reviews every warranty claim and may ask for photos of the defect.
If the claim is approved, we repair the item, replace it, or refund it.
